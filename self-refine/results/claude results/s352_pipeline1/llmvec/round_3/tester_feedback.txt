The candidate manually unrolls by 5 but only processes indices `i, i+1, i+2, i+3, i+4` — this is only correct if `LEN_1D` is always a multiple of 5, which may not hold, causing wrong results.

  ```c
  for (int i = 0; i < LEN_1D; i++) {
      dot += a[i] * b[i];
  }
  ```
- Remove the manual unroll-by-5 (`i += 5`) and the multi-term accumulation; let the compiler auto-vectorize the simple loop instead.
